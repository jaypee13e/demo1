        Questions
        
            
        Quick answers before you send a message.
        
          
        
        
        
        
        
          
        
            
        
              How do I place an order?
              
        
                Send us a direct message on Instagram or Messenger with the product, quantity and
                your design. The Start an order page writes that message for you.
              
        
            
        
            
        
              Do you ship nationwide?
              
        Yes. We ship nationwide, to your door anywhere in the Philippines.
        
            
        
            
        
              How much does it cost?
              
        
                The price depends on the product, quantity and design. Send us your details and
                we’ll confirm the price with you before we print.
              
        
            
        
            
        
              Can I order just one piece?
              
        Yes. We print one custom mug as happily as a whole team’s shirts.
        
            
        
            
        
              Do I need a finished design?
              
        
                No. A rough concept is enough to start. We prepare the layout and you approve it
                before printing.
              
        
            
        
            
        
              What is the difference between sublimation and DTF?
              
        
                Sublimation prints the design into the surface of items like mugs and tumblers, so
                it doesn’t peel. DTF uses a full-color transfer applied to fabric items like shirts,
                caps and bags.
              
        
            
        
            
        
              How long will my order take?
              
        
                It depends on the product and quantity. Ask for the turnaround time when you send
                your message, and we’ll give you a date.
